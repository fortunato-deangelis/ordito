# ORDITO Adoption Plan — Days 0–90

A concrete operational plan for teams adopting ORDITO. The goal is not to implement everything at once, but to build the habit layer by layer — starting with the highest-leverage, lowest-disruption changes.

> **Principle**: start with the minimum that demonstrates value, then expand. A framework that gets used imperfectly is better than one that never starts.

---

## Days 0–30: Stabilize Intake and Discovery

### Goal

Make the first two phases (Intake and Discovery) explicit and artifact-driven. This immediately reduces the chaos of "where did this feature request come from?" and "why are we building this?"

### What to do

**Week 1 — Baseline**
- [ ] Read the [Manifesto](../MANIFESTO.md) with the team (30 min)
- [ ] Identify your existing analogues: what do you currently call Initiative Charters? Feature Requests? Where do they live?
- [ ] Choose your first initiative to run through ORDITO (ideally: medium complexity, ongoing, not finished)
- [ ] Map it to ORDITO phases retroactively — this is the fastest way to see what's missing

**Week 2 — First artifact**
- [ ] Create the Initiative Charter for your chosen initiative using the schema fields from `schemas/artifact.schema.json`
- [ ] Identify who is the Sponsor, Product Lead, and Engineering Lead for this initiative
- [ ] Run existing Feature Requests through the Intake Coach prompt template (from `04-ai-service-mesh/prompts/intake-coach.md`)
- [ ] Hold your first Intake Review ritual (45 min, see `02-operating-model/rituals.md`)

**Week 3 — Discovery artifact**
- [ ] Write the Feature Brief Pack for your chosen initiative
- [ ] Activate Brief Builder and Research Synthesizer AI services
- [ ] Schedule your first G1 review

**Week 4 — First gate**
- [ ] Run your first G1 gate decision
- [ ] Log the decision in the artifact's `decision_log`
- [ ] Identify what was hard about the Brief Pack that you want to improve

### KPI at Day 30

- At least 1 initiative with a complete Initiative Charter and Feature Brief Pack conforming to `artifact.schema.json`
- G1 decision logged in the artifact
- Team can name the owner of each phase for the pilot initiative

### AI services active at Day 30

- `intake-coach` (phase 1)
- `brief-builder` and `research-synthesizer` (phase 3)

## Days 31–60: Connect Design, Engineering, and QA

### Goal

Activate G2 and make the handoff from Discovery to Build explicit. This reduces the most common source of rework: incomplete briefs that arrive in engineering.

### What to do

**Week 5–6 — Activate G2**
- [ ] For your pilot initiative, run Solution Design using the Design Spec + TDN templates
- [ ] Activate Design Critic and Solution Mapper AI services
- [ ] Hold your first G2 review (45 min)
- [ ] For Scale mode: activate Dependency Mapper and schedule Staff Engineer sign-off

**Week 7 — Connect QA**
- [ ] Activate the Code Review Agent for your first PR in the pilot initiative
- [ ] Activate Test Case Generator from the ACs in the Feature Brief Pack
- [ ] Hold your first Sprint Sync (30 min)

**Week 8 — Formalize**
- [ ] Write your first Tracking Plan (events + KPIs) in the Feature Brief Pack
- [ ] Confirm Data Analytics is reviewing it before release
- [ ] Expand ORDITO to a second initiative — the pattern is now repeatable

### KPI at Day 60

- G2 gate run at least once with documented outcome
- Code Review Agent active on at least one PR
- Two initiatives tracked in ORDITO (at least one with active artifacts)
- Team has run all 5 core rituals at least once

### AI services active at Day 60

- All Day 30 services
- `design-critic` and `solution-mapper` (phase 4)
- `code-review-agent` (phase 5)
- `test-case-generator` (phase 6)

## Days 61–90: Close the Release and Learning Loop

### Goal

Close the first complete ORDITO cycle — from Initiative Charter to Impact Review. This is where the compounding value of the framework begins: the learning loop reveals what worked and what didn't.

### What to do

**Week 9–10 — Release readiness**
- [ ] Activate Release Verifier for your pilot initiative's release gate
- [ ] Produce the complete Release Checklist (all ACs verified)
- [ ] Run the Release Gate ritual (20–30 min)
- [ ] Document your rollout tier (dark / beta / full) and reason

**Week 11 — First Impact Review**
- [ ] At 7 days post-release: first quick check on KPIs
- [ ] At 30 days: run Dashboard Narrator on your telemetry data
- [ ] Produce the Impact Review and run the Learning Ritual
- [ ] Record the Learning Gate decision (Value Confirmed / Pivot / Kill)

**Week 12 — ORDITO Retrospective**
- [ ] Run your first ORDITO Retrospective (60–90 min)
- [ ] Review override rate, artifact conformance, learning loop completion
- [ ] Identify the top 2–3 friction points and decide: adjust the process or submit an RFC
- [ ] Update CHANGELOG with v1.x adoption notes

### KPI at Day 90

- At least 1 complete ORDITO cycle (Charter → Impact Review) for the pilot initiative
- All AI services from the registry active at least once
- ORDITO Retrospective completed with documented improvements
- Team can run a new initiative end-to-end without external guidance

### AI services active at Day 90

- All services from Days 0–60
- `release-verifier` (phase 6)
- `dashboard-narrator` (phase 8)

## Common failure modes

**"We don't have time for this"**
Start with just two artifacts: Initiative Charter and Feature Brief Pack. Skip everything else until those feel natural. The framework is a spectrum — start at 20% coverage and expand.

**"We already have a process"**
Map your existing process to ORDITO phases. Keep what works, add what's missing. ORDITO does not require replacing your tools or ceremonies — it layers on top of them.

**"The AI suggestions are often wrong"**
That's expected. The AI services are meant to accelerate, not to replace judgment. Track overrides in the `decision_log` — they are the most valuable tuning signal you have. After 30 days, review which services are overridden most and adjust their prompts.

**"It takes too long to fill in all the fields"**
The schema has required fields and optional fields. Start with required fields only: `artifact_id`, `artifact_type`, `owner_role`, `status`, `objective`, `last_reviewed_at`. Add optional fields as the team becomes comfortable.

---

## What success looks like at 90 days

- The team has at least one complete ORDITO cycle as evidence
- Gate decisions are documented, not just made verbally
- AI service outputs are reviewed and overrides are logged
- The framework retrospective has produced at least one concrete improvement
- The team can onboard a new member to ORDITO in a half-day session
