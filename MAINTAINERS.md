ORDITO maintainers are responsible for the framework's direction, RFC review, community care, and repository quality.

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no formal application process. You become a maintainer after:

1. At least 5 substantial accepted contributions (RFCs, examples, improvements)
2. Active participation in reviewing other contributors' PRs (at least 10 reviews)
3. Demonstrated alignment with manifesto principles
4. Explicit invitation from current maintainers

Maintainers can be removed for prolonged inactivity (>6 months without contributions or reviews) or for [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) violations.

## Decision-making process

- **Operational decisions** (PR review, issue triage): any maintainer can decide
- **Evolution decisions** (RFC accepted/rejected): consensus among active maintainers
- **Controversial decisions**: majority, with motivated veto possible from the Lead Maintainer for manifesto consistency
- **Manifesto modifications**: requires community vote in addition to maintainer consensus
